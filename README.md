# Diwali_Sales_Analysis
# 🪔 Diwali Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project performs an in-depth **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Diwali
season sales data to identify trends, customer segments, and product performance
insights — with the goal of enhancing **customer experience** and maximizing
**revenue during festive campaigns**.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer demographics (age, gender, region, occupation)
- Identify top-performing product categories and items
- Uncover purchasing patterns across states and customer segments
- Provide data-driven recommendations to optimize marketing and inventory

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- **Jupyter Notebook**
- **Data Visualization** for actionable business insights

## 📂 Dataset
Sales transaction records from the Diwali festive season including customer
demographics, product categories, and purchase amounts.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Most buyers are married women aged 26–35 from UP, Maharashtra & Karnataka
- Customers working in IT, Healthcare & Aviation are the highest spenders
- Food, Clothing & Electronics are the top-selling product categories

## 📈 Outcome
Insights from this analysis can help businesses plan **targeted marketing
